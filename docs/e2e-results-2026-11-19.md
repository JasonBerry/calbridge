## Findings

- **Mac Calendar can revive stale events.** After the cleanup, the Mac kept a deleted event in its local cache. Deleting it on the Mac uploaded it back to iCloud as a *declined* (hidden) event. Check icloud.com before deleting something only the Mac shows. Restarting the sync services on this macOS: quit Calendar, `killall dataaccessd calaccessd`.
- **Google's RSVP emails landed in Gmail Spam** because the custom domain failed DMARC (`p=quarantine`): Google signed with its default `gappssmtp.com` DKIM key and SPF did not list Google. Enabling Workspace DKIM for the domain (and adding Google to SPF) fixed it during the trial. Not a calbridge issue; goes in the README operations section (M14).
- Verified against real Google (previously UNVERIFIED): instance-id suffix format and per-occurrence RSVP to `<master>_<key>` for timed series; new iCalUID on "this and following". Still unverified: the all-day forms of both.
- Cosmetic: `/status` `last_cycle` field names were capitalised; now snake_case.
